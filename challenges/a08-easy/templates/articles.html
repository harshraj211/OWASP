Developer Bulletin: Third-Party Plugin Signing Standard

Novus CMS requires all production extensions to provide an integrity manifest manifest.json inside the ZIP bundle. Production installations enforce digital signatures certified by the Novus Root CA.

Testing Exceptions: For local staging and test environments, developers can configure verification_mode: "developer_bypass" with environment: "sandbox" to bypass public CA signature verification during extension prototyping.
