Developer Bulletin: Third-Party Plugin Signing Standard

Novus CMS requires third-party plugins to provide an integrity manifest manifest.json inside the ZIP archive. For internal sandbox testing, the signature verifier supports verification_mode: "developer_bypass" or self-signed verification when public_key.pem is bundled in the archive.
