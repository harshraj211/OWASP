# A06:2025 - Insecure Design (Easy)

## Negative Price Purchase

Northstar Market gives every new customer a small promotional wallet. A limited-edition travel case has appeared in the catalog, but its price is far beyond your available balance.

Obtain the **Redline Travel Case** without paying its listed price and recover the access code included with the fulfilled order.

- Category: A06:2025 - Insecure Design
- Difficulty: Easy
- Estimated time: 10-20 minutes
- Flag format: `CTF{...}`
- Starting balance: `$75.00`

Only interact with the challenge through its web interface and HTTP requests.
