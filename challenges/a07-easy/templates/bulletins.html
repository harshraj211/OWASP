SEC-2026-04: Dispatch Key Hygiene & Brute-Force Rate Limiting

Security Directive

An internal audit revealed that field dispatch personnel frequently configure predictable terminal passwords based on common annual operational templates (such as AeroFleet2025!, FlightOps2026!, Dispatch2026!, or LAXOps2026!).

To mitigate unauthorized access attempts, the dispatch login portal enforces IP-based rate limiting (blocking addresses exceeding 3 consecutive failed attempts).

OPS-2026-03: Transpacific Diplomatic Cargo Authorization

Flight Operations

Flight AF-802 carries diplomatic priority air freight. The electronic diplomatic clearance manifest is restricted to Chief Flight Dispatchers and requires authentication via the Dispatch Console.
