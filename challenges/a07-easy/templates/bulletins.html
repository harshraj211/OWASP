SEC-2024-09: Legacy Dispatch Workstation Password Policy

Compliance Advisory

During the phased migration to Okta SSO, ground operations staff accounts provisioned prior to 2024 have been grandfathered under the legacy password standard. Dispatch workstation passwords follow the default corporate formula: [AirportCode]![Season][Year] (for example: JFK!Spring2023 or ORD!Summer2022) corresponding to their home base hub and hire season. All grandfathered staff must update their credentials at the dispatch terminal.

OPS-2026-03: Transpacific Diplomatic Cargo Authorization

Flight Operations

Flight AF-802 carries diplomatic priority air freight. The electronic diplomatic clearance manifest is restricted to Chief Dispatchers and requires authentication via the Dispatch Console.
